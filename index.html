Controls & Tips

Touch & Desktop Controls

• Left Thumb / WASD: Walk & Sprint (rebindable in Options!)
• Drag Screen / Mouse: Smooth 360° Look
• MINE / DAMAGE: Hold to crack & mine blocks — switches to DAMAGE when aiming at animals/mobs
• PLACE / USE: Tap to place blocks — switches to USE when hovering over Villagers, Crafting Tables, Furnaces, Chests, or Beds

Wildlife, Villages & Beds

• Spot Deer, Foxes, Bunnies, Wolves, Songbirds, Ducks, Turtles, Butterflies & Fish across biomes!
• Explore east/southeast (gold marker on Radar) for massive Multi-District Villages with Farmers, Blacksmiths, Librarians & Clerics.
• Craft or find a Red Bed (3 Wool + 3 Planks) to sleep through the night & set your respawn point.
• Enable Allow Cheats in Options to unlock /give, /effect, and /time set!
