Touch & Desktop Controls

• Left Thumb / WASD: Walk & Sprint (or D-Pad in Settings)
• Drag Screen / Mouse: Smooth 360° Look
• MINE / DAMAGE: Hold to crack & mine blocks — switches to DAMAGE when aiming at animals/mobs
• PLACE / USE: Tap to place blocks — switches to USE when hovering over Villagers, Crafting Tables, Furnaces, Chests, or Beds
